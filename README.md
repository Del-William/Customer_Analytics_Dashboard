# Customer Analytics Dashboard

**Overview**  
This Power BI dashboard provides insights into customer demographics, purchasing behavior, and revenue trends. It is designed to help businesses identify high-value customer segments, understand revenue distribution, and make data-driven decisions.

**Key Features**
- Interactive visualizations of customer metrics including total customers, average age, and revenue distribution
- Customer segmentation by age group, gender, and parental status
- Highlighting of top-performing customers and revenue contributions
- Filter functionality to view data by country and interactive theme switching (light/dark mode)
- Easy-to-read KPIs and charts for quick business insights

**Tools & Technologies**
- Power BI Desktop
- Excel (for sample dataset)
- Interactive charts, slicers, and KPI visuals

**Insights**
- Customers aged 31–40 contribute the highest revenue across six countries
- Customers with children make up 71% of the customer base and account for significant revenue
- Balanced gender distribution among revenue contributors

**Usage**
- Open the `.pbix` file in Power BI Desktop
- Use the filters and theme switcher to explore different segments
- Hover over charts to view detailed metrics and insights
